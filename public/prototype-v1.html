How it plays
  • Drips form along the gutter and fall on the beat of the lo-fi loop. Each drip point plays a note, so more drip points make more music.
  • Drops wear down the top layer. Clear a whole row and you sink one level deeper. It never ends, and the layers get tougher: dirt → clay → stone → iron → crystal → ruins.
  • If a drip point fills up faster than the beat can release it, the drops merge into one bigger drop.
  • Acid rain eats through iron. Lightning chains through neighbouring metal. Storms roll in on their own after depth 8.
  • A snail sometimes wanders by. Rain on it (or tap it) for a bonus.
  • From depth 40 you can reset with Clear Skies to earn rainbow shards (+15% damage and loot each).
  • Progress saves in this browser.
