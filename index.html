How it plays
  • Drips form along the gutter and fall on the beat. Each drip point plays a note, so more drip points make more music.
  • Clear a whole row to sink one level deeper. Layers go dirt → clay → stone → iron → crystal → ruins → obsidian → fossil → magma → starstone → the core.
  • Break blocks quickly one after another to build a combo for bonus loot.
  • If a drip point overflows the beat, its drops merge into one heavier drop.
  • Metal takes ×3 from acid and passes lightning on. Crystal shatters on crits. Rain cools magma for +50%.
  • Upgrades are capped each season. Clear Skies halves your levels, raises every cap and pays rainbow shards for the skill tree.
